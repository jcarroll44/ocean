# DayBuoy privacy policy

Effective October 11, 2026. Operated by Jacob Carroll.

DayBuoy shows forecasts for Inlet Beach, Florida. It does not request GPS location, create an app account, show ads, or use advertising trackers.

## On your device

The app stores recent forecasts, the last NWS surf report and preferences locally. The widget keeps its own short-lived forecast cache. Removing the app clears its app storage; website storage can be cleared in browser settings.

## Forecasts and hosting

The iPhone app includes its interface and ocean scene in the installed app. It requests forecast data from Open-Meteo, NOAA and the National Weather Service using the configured beach coordinates, not your GPS position. The website is hosted separately and requires sign-in during private testing.

Network providers receive request information, including IP addresses, and may keep operational/security logs under their policies. See [Open-Meteo terms](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms), [NOAA privacy](https://www.noaa.gov/privacy) and [hosting provider privacy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/).

## App diagnostics and updates

Native beta builds configured with Sentry send crash reports, error details, app/OS/device technical information and failed-feed events to diagnose defects. We do not intentionally attach names, email addresses, GPS location, local preferences, screenshots or recordings. Session replay, performance tracing, user-action breadcrumbs and advertising tracking are disabled. Native crash reports can include technical device identifiers and stack information. Providers receive network metadata. Retention follows the settings of the service account and provider policy.

Native builds configured with Capgo check for compatible web-asset updates and download them. Update requests include app/version/platform information and an installation identifier. Optional update statistics are disabled. The private upload key stays with the developer and is not in the app. No Sentry or Capgo account is contacted by this integration until configured in that build.

See [Sentry privacy](https://sentry.io/privacy/) and [Capgo privacy](https://capgo.app/privacy/).

## Sharing and TestFlight

Sharing is initiated by you. Production app builds do not automatically upload screenshots, clips or performance-test results. Private development capture tools are separate. Apple may collect TestFlight diagnostics and feedback under its terms.

## Contact

Use the [DayBuoy support tracker](https://github.com/jcarroll44/ocean/issues) for privacy questions; do not post sensitive information there. Material policy changes will update this page and its effective date.
