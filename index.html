What is unchanged—and what this proves

Tidewater's vendored source files remain byte-for-byte matched to revision 4811ba48…. The host supplies our resting camera, a beach with a 0.105 slope, test wave height and direction. Wave shapes, curling lips, foam, caustics and water shading run through the original code. A documented spray adapter limits active ring slots, compute dispatch and drawing. Quality changes do not change swell height or period.

Phone tuning: output DPR 1.0–1.5, automatic wave LOD, 512–32,768 active spray slots, reduced refraction resolution and reflection refresh, smaller phone reflection cube and cloud buffer. The four 256² FFT cascades, breaking lip and 768² swash remain. No WebGL fallback. Auto checks rolling GPU completions, steps down after sustained misses, and reports if minimum quality still cannot reach 30 fps.

The beach geometry is 0.105. Tidewater's internal swash calibration retains its original hard-coded 0.066 nominal slope to keep the ocean source unchanged. That difference is documented, not silently patched. The height selector drives shore-wave amplitude as feet × 0.3048 ÷ 2; offshore spectrum energy is scaled proportionally. It is not a validated significant-wave-height calibration.
