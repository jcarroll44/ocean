DayBuoy

Privacy policy

Effective October 11, 2026. DayBuoy is operated by Jacob Carroll.

What this version does

DayBuoy displays forecasts for Inlet Beach, Florida. It does not request your device location, create an app account, show ads, or include advertising trackers or analytics SDKs.

Information stored on your device

The last retrieved forecast and preferences such as skin-tone and SPF selections are saved locally in the app’s web storage. These preferences are not sent to a DayBuoy account or database. Removing the app clears its app-local storage; website storage can be cleared in your browser settings.

Network requests

The app downloads its interface and scene from its hosted site. Hosting and network providers necessarily receive network information such as your IP address and request details and may retain operational or security logs under their own policies. We do not control those providers’ retention schedules.

Your device requests forecast data from Open-Meteo and tide and weather information from NOAA and the National Weather Service. Those services receive the request, your IP address and the configured beach coordinates—not your device’s GPS location. Their own policies apply: Open-Meteo terms, NOAA privacy, and hosting provider privacy.

Sharing and beta testing

A share or save action is initiated by you. Public DayBuoy does not automatically upload screenshots, recordings or test results. Separate private development tools are not part of this release. If installed through TestFlight, Apple may collect beta feedback and diagnostics under its own terms.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, use the public DayBuoy support tracker. Do not post sensitive personal information there. Material changes to this policy will be reflected here with an updated effective date.
